# The Ted Lasso Way

I have just watched the finale of the third season of Ted Lasso. I can’t tell you how incredible I felt. It has been a remarkable watch. I absolutely loved it. The way everything came together in the finale, I have to give the writers props. Besides, the character development throughout the seasons has been exceptional. Almost every character has gone through a personal change, and it has been captivating to observe that. I would rather not get into every character, but I want to talk a bit about Ted Lasso.

Ted expressed to Trent Crimm, who has been writing a book about the football club A.F.C. Richmond in the last season, that the title of the book shouldn’t be _The Lasso Way_; thus, Trent changes it to _The Richmond Way_. In my eyes, conversely, it has been about Ted. Hence, I want to praise him for the person he is and what he has done for A.F.C. Richmond.

I never expected myself to absolutely adore a personage from a TV show, but I do. Ted has made me rethink how I handle certain situations and taught me to be a better person.

There isn’t really a conclusion to this, but I would advise you to watch Ted Lasso. It will change you.
