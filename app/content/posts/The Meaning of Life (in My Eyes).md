# The Meaning of Life (in My Eyes)

I think the meaning of life is purely an egostatistical question. I don’t think there is a meaning inherently, before you were born, that has been given to you. Pessimistically, I have suggested that life has no meaning by now. Therefore, you have to figure out your meaning.

My meaning of life is to be happy. For some people, they want to be remembered after they have died. I don’t really care about it. I see beauty in the to-be-forgotten. However, it can be a meaning of life. Whether you want it or not, you will be neglected anyway.  
Another meaning of life for me is to leave a positive impact on society. I would love to believe that society can become a better place, and I’ll take that thought to my grave.
